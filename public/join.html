FAQs

Common questions

I want to join, but I have no experience in digital marketing!

That's completely fine — most of our members join without prior marketing experience. We provide lectures and mentorship from officers so you can learn as you go, on real client work.

Do I need to be a UT Austin student to join?

HMS is a student organization based at the University of Texas at Austin, and our teams are made up of current UT Austin students.

How much time does being a member take?

Teams of four work with a client each semester, combining regular team meetings with independent work on strategy, content, and reporting.

When does the next application open?

Our application for Fall 2026 is opening soon — follow us on Instagram or LinkedIn for the announcement and application link.
