Privacy Policy

The basics of how Hooked Media Strategy handles your information.

Hooked Media Strategy ("we," "our," or "us") is a student organization at The University of Texas at Austin dedicated to respecting visitor privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and manage personal information from website visitors.

Information We Collect

  • Contact details you submit voluntarily through forms or mailing list signups
  • Usage data including IP addresses, browser information, pages visited, and time spent on site via analytics tools

Why We Collect It

Data is collected to respond to inquiries, enhance website functionality, analyze visitor behavior, and showcase student team accomplishments.

Use and Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We may share anonymized data with university advisors for evaluation purposes, or disclose information when legally required. Third-party tools such as Google Analytics and Mailchimp operate under their own privacy policies.

Your Rights

You may access, correct, or delete your personal information, or opt out of communications, by contacting us.

Children's Privacy

This site is not intended for children under 13, and parental consent is required for any collection of a minor's data.

Policy Updates

Changes to this policy will be posted here with an updated date.
