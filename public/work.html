Our Work

Partnerships that made an impact.

A look at the organizations we've worked with, semester by semester.

Feb 2025 – May 2025

Change 1

Change 1 is a nonprofit supporting youth aging out of foster care. We partnered with their core team and the UT Austin PR division to strengthen outreach to foster youth and the case managers who serve them.

Sep 2025 – Dec 2025

Perplexity AI

Perplexity AI is a conversational answer engine redefining information discovery. We partnered with their team to launch the Comet browser at UT Austin, combining viral social strategies with face-to-face engagement to drive brand trust and user adoption.

Jan 2026 – May 2026

Towit

Towit is a peer-to-peer marketplace revolutionizing the trailer rental industry. We're partnering with their team to execute a comprehensive brand transition and niche-market expansion, leveraging SEO-driven content and targeted social strategies to build user trust within the equestrian and local hauling communities.
