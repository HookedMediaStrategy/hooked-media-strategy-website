Our Mission

Free digital marketing, powered by student talent.

We are Hooked Media Strategy (HMS), a student-run organization at the University of Texas at Austin providing free digital marketing services to small businesses and nonprofits.

The Dual Mission

Empowering organizations. Equipping students.

We pursue two goals at once: helping local organizations grow through smart digital marketing strategy, and giving students hands-on, career-ready experience they can't get in a classroom.

Every semester, student teams of four partner directly with a client to build and execute a real social media and digital PR strategy, from research and content to reporting on what actually worked.

We're expanding beyond social media into broader digital marketing services as our team and expertise grow.

Founding Story

How HMS started

Co-founder Gia Nguyen conceived HMS while collaborating with Sabrina Chew and Shikha Mehta on an ADHD productivity app and managing social media for various organizations.

Gia realized how transferable the skills gained through social media management are, especially in digital marketing.

Eager to provide other students with hands-on learning opportunities while giving back to the community, Gia shared her vision and passion for digital marketing with Sabrina Chew and Shikha Mehta, who quickly jumped on board. Sharing a passion for service, the trio decided to offer their expertise free of charge to small businesses and nonprofits.

Thus, Hooked Media Strategy was born: a name that reflects their mission to craft strategies that not only connect brands to their audience but also hook them with compelling, impactful content.
