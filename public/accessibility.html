Accessibility Statement

Hooked Media Strategy is a student-run organization at the University of Texas at Austin, dedicated to following the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) to ensure our website is perceivable, operable, and understandable for all users, including those with disabilities.

What we've implemented

  • Used accessibility evaluation tools to identify issues
  • Set the site language for screen readers
  • Defined clear content and reading order
  • Structured headings consistently
  • Added alternative text to meaningful images
  • Met WCAG 2.1 contrast standards
  • Minimized motion and flashing content
  • Ensured videos, audio, and files are accessible or have alternatives

Partial compliance

Our "Our Work" page contains third-party embedded content that is outside of our direct control.

Contact

As a student-run organization, we are actively learning and applying accessibility best practices to improve the experience for everyone. If you encounter an accessibility issue, contact our accessibility coordinator at hookedmediastrategy@gmail.com.
